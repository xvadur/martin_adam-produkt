## Behaviour

Six accessible service disclosures switch the scene and actual service photograph, support arrow keys and retain all text in HTML. Five manually selectable process steps; on mobile all descriptions are open. Three full quotes are manually paged, no autoplay. Global visible pause and reduced-motion support. Canvas paints only when visible and stops in a background tab. Mobile menu supports Escape and closes when a link is selected. All seven contact fields have labels and original requiredness; submission prepares a mailto draft with native validation and does not claim message delivery. Detail links lead to the original website.
